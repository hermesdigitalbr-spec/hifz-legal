The service

Hifz schedules your daily memorization: a new lesson (Sabaq), daily revision of recent lessons (Sabqi) and spaced revision of older portions (Manzil). Schedules, strength estimates and certificates are study aids. They are not an ijazah and do not replace a qualified teacher.
