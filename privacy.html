Privacy Policy

Hifz · Qur'an memorization

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: Hifz does not collect your personal data. Your plan, progress, slips and recordings stay on your device, and, if you sign in with Apple, in your own iCloud account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your memorization record: plan, lessons, revision schedule, slip log, sittings, teacher notes and halaqa details.
  • Recordings of your recitation, made only when you press record. They never leave your device unless you share them yourself (for example with your teacher) through the share sheet.
  • Approximate location, only if you allow it, rounded to about 1 km and used on the device to calculate prayer times for your reminder. It is never sent anywhere.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, the app receives an anonymous identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email. With iCloud backup on, a copy of your record is stored in your private iCloud key-value storage so it can be restored on a new device. We cannot see it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares anonymous, aggregated sales reports with developers.

Third-party content

When you play recitation, the audio files are downloaded from EveryAyah.com (and word-by-word audio from the Quran.com content network). As with any web request, those servers can see your IP address. No account or personal data is sent.

Notifications and microphone

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Microphone access is used only while you record.

Children

Hifz is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings › Account › Delete account erases everything on the device and in your iCloud backup. Deleting the app also removes the data stored on the device. You can export your record first (Settings › Account › Export my data).
